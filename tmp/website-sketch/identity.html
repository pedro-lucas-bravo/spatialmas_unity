Quiet structure. A vivid encounter.

Forest-black surfaces and pale mineral tones give the simulations room to breathe. Coral identifies an intervention or an agent worth noticing. The identity avoids relying on a headset silhouette, leaving space for desktop, XR and future applications.

One system, two modes.

Space Grotesk gives headlines and the wordmark a precise but human character. DM Sans supports readable explanations. The future Quarto handbook can use the same palette and typography with light reading surfaces.

What to review

Does it feel credible for a research lab and inviting for a creative team? Is the mark distinctive enough? Do you prefer this organic, tactile direction or a cooler, more explicitly technical identity?

What is provisional

This is one coherent design direction, not a final brand. The logo needs further small-size and reproduction refinement. Fonts currently load from Google Fonts; final hosting can serve them locally. No trademark clearance has been performed.
